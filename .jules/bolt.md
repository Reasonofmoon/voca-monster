## 2024-06-03 - O(1) Capturing Group Highlighting
**Learning:** In the `AnalyzedContent` component, text highlighting was being performed via an O(N*M) nested lookup `highlightWords.some(...)` inside `parts.map` where N is parts count and M is vocabulary words count. However, since the parts array is generated by `String.prototype.split(regex)` where `regex` contains a single capturing group for matches, JavaScript guarantees that the matched terms will appear at odd indices (1, 3, 5...) and the unmatched surrounding text at even indices (0, 2, 4...).
**Action:** Replace `Array.prototype.some()` lookups inside mapping functions with an O(1) mathematical index check (`index % 2 === 1`) when dealing with arrays created from splitting strings via a capturing regex. Always combine this with `React.memo` and `React.useMemo` to memoize the regex compilation and resulting split array.
